# Coursework — LLM Society

This directory contains written coursework and related presentation.

# Abstract

This paper analyzes the application of large language models (LLMs) in society simulation systems. It goes over the theoretical foundations of societal models, the evolution of agent-based systems, and their use in social interaction modeling. Further on, several methods for applying LLMs are examined: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), fine-tuning, and prompt engineering. Technological and implementation challenges are also discussed, such as the model's inherent bias, logical inconsistencies, and potentially incorrect implementation of simulation systems or their parts. Lastly, two studies are analyzed, both describing social simulation systems based on LLMs. The studies show that LLMs can serve as effective tools for modeling complex social phenomena however, it is essential to consider technological challenges and potential implementation errors in the system’s components.
